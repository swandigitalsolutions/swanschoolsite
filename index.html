About SwanSchool

A school that feels like a second home.

Founded in 1998 by a group of teachers and parents, SwanSchool has grown from 60 students in a rented bungalow to a 14-acre campus. What has not changed is the idea we started with: children learn best when they feel safe, known and challenged.

Curiosity

Inquiry-led lessons where questions matter as much as answers.

Character

Weekly values circles, service projects and student leadership.

Craft

Practice in labs, studios and on the field, not only in notebooks.

Care

A counsellor for every 300 students and a mentor for every child.

Academics

One path, four stages.

Our curriculum follows CBSE and NEP 2020 stages, with extra time for reading, coding and the arts at every level.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions.

What is the age criteria for Nursery admission?

Children should be 3 years old by 31 May of the admission year. For Grade 1, the child should be 6 years old by 31 May, as per Karnataka guidelines.

Which documents are needed?

Birth certificate, Aadhaar of child and parents, two passport photos, the previous school’s report card and a Transfer Certificate for Grade 2 and above.

Do you offer transport to my area?

Our 42 buses cover most of south and east Bengaluru within a 15 km radius. Share your area in the enquiry form and we will confirm the nearest stop.

Is there support for children with learning differences?

Yes. Our learning support centre has two special educators and an occupational therapist who work with class teachers on individual education plans.

What are the school timings?

Nursery to UKG: 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM. Grades 1 to 12: 8:00 AM to 3:15 PM, Monday to Friday. Saturdays are for clubs, tours and events.
